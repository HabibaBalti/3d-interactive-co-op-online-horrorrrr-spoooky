# Walkthrough (spoilers)

How every puzzle works and how to solve it. Answers marked _random_ change every game (they come
from the room's seed), so this explains **where to look**, not the answer. Only the 3:17 board
has fixed answers, given in full at the end.

The house: front door at the south end of the hall, the basement door at its north end (the bolt
is on the hall side). Dining room and kitchen to the west, living room and the piano/study rooms
to the east. The grandfather clock and the photo wall are in the hall.

## Act 1 — The House

**1. The grandfather clock**

- Sam: his clock stopped at 3:17. He tells Nora.
- Nora: her clock still runs. Use it → set the hour and minute with the arrows → **pull the
  chain**. It strikes, the case opens, and the **wind key** is inside (take it).
- From now on both clocks read 3:17.

**2. The floorboards** _(random board)_

- Sam: in the hall, near the front door, three boards are missing. Under one of them a child
  carved a diamond into the joist. Sam counts which board it is, from the dining-room (west) side.
- Nora: her row of ten boards all look the same. She counts the same way and **pries** that
  board. Under it is little Sam's **toy rabbit**.
- Nora leaves the rabbit somewhere: the clock compartment, the hall table by the front door, or
  the piano bench. She tells Sam where.
- Sam finds it there, decades older. Taking it gives him the **piano key** (it was in the
  battery hatch).

**3. The lullaby** _(random colours)_

- Nora: wind the music box (needs the wind key), then look at it. Coloured marks on the drum
  light up one at a time: six of them. She reads out the order.
- Sam: **uncover** the piano, **unlock** it (piano key), and play. Four keys carry little Sam's
  coloured stickers. Press them in Nora's order.
- The lullaby plays in both houses, and Act 2 begins.

## Act 2 — Something Else Is Listening

**4. The photo wall** _(random order)_

- Sam: his photos hang in an order Nora's don't. They are faded, scratched and washed out, but
  still recognisable. He describes each one, left to right.
- Nora: **rehang** the photos. Click two to swap them, then **hang them** when the order matches.
- Sam's porch photo falls off its nail. A **tape** is taped behind it.

**5. The tape and Dad's keys** _(random hiding place)_

- Sam: put the tape in the TV in the living room and watch it to the end (about 12 seconds). It
  is the kitchen at 2:50 a.m.: Dad hiding his keys while Mum watches. He tells Nora where the keys go.
- Nora: search that place in her kitchen. The choices are the cookie jar, the bread bin, the
  drawer, and the top of the fridge.
- The keys only turn up after Sam has watched the tape.

**6. Hide-and-seek** _(random safe hiding place)_

- Taking the keys wakes something in the basement. The lights die, and it comes up the stairs.
- Sam: one of little Sam's crayon drawings shows the girl hiding and the tall thing walking past.
  It is on the hall wall by the stair cupboard. Which hiding place it shows (dining table, sofa,
  or study desk) is the safe one.
- Nora: hide there (use it), and don't move. Walk, don't run: it hears running. Press E to leave
  once it has gone back down.
- If it finds her: a scare, she wakes by the front door, and a few seconds later it comes again.

**7. The mirror**

- Both: go to the hall mirror (on the stair cupboard). Each of you sees the other timeline in it,
  and a faint version of your partner if they're standing there.
- Stand in front of it at the same time. Sam sees something standing right behind Nora that isn't
  on her screen.
- Sam must tell Nora **not to turn around**. She has to keep facing the mirror for about 12
  seconds. If she turns, it's there (a scare), and they try again.
- When she holds still, Act 3 begins.

## Act 3 — The Basement

**8. The flood valves** _(random order)_

- Sam: slide the bolt on the basement door (it moves now) and go down. On the boiler wall are
  four coloured valves. Painted above each one are tally strokes, 1 to 4. That is the order:
  one stroke first.
- Nora: **unlock** the basement door with Dad's keys and go down. The basement starts flooding.
  At the valves on the boiler wall, **turn** opens the four wheels: close them in Sam's order.
- A wrong valve sends a surge: all the valves reopen and the water jumps. If the water closes over
  her head for a few seconds, she drowns and the act starts again.
- When all four are shut, the water stops. Something upstairs shuts the basement door.

**9. The bolted door and the 3:17 board** _(fixed answers)_

- Nora: climb back up the stairs. The door at the top is **bolted from the other side**.
- A board appears on the basement's east wall in both timelines. Use it to **reconstruct** the
  night. Each of you fills only your own half; you see only how full your partner's half is.
- Both **lock in**. The candles show how much is right, but only in whole groups of three, so
  guessing doesn't work. A wrong board unlocks you both, scares you, and the night gets worse.

| Nora's half: where was everyone at 3:17? |                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Ruth (Mum)                               | upstairs (stairs icon) |
| Walter (Dad)                             | living room (TV icon)  |
| Nora                                     | basement (water icon)  |
| Sam (little brother)                     | hall (door icon)       |

| Sam's half        |                                  |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| What happened     | the bolt                         |
| Who did it        | Sam (the little boy in the cap)  |
| Why it was hidden | to protect him (adult and child) |

The clues for this are in the memory echoes from all three acts, the tape, the drawings, the
photos, the lore lines, and the answering machine.

## Endings

- **Truth**: all seven correct. Then Sam goes back upstairs. The house slams the basement door
  and bolts it, the way it was that night. He **unbolts** it. Nora's door opens into light and she
  walks up; Sam's front door opens. Credits, then **play again** starts a fresh night.
- **The lie**: answer "she ran away" (the running-figure icon) for what happened, or for why.
  The lights go out, something walks toward you saying it, and the night begins again, worse.
- **Wrong board**: the candles show how many groups of three were right. You both unlock
  and try again. The loop count goes up, lamps die, and more goes wrong around the house.
